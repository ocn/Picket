# 03 — Evolve separate Battle Reports with contextual losses

**What to build:** Turn live candidates into inspectable single-system Battle Reports whose contextual losses, quiet closure and later revisions remain explicit.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Discover a live candidate from an opted-in source

**Status:** draft — awaiting breakdown approval

- [ ] Include observed combat in an explicit evolving system/time window, including retained earlier in-window evidence and nonmatching Contextual Killmails; label source matches and context separately.
- [ ] Preserve stable local identities, unique loss counts, participant/ship summaries and inclusion reasons in both persisted queries and the private report detail.
- [ ] Close provisionally after twenty minutes of healthy quiet; define and test exact event-window boundary conventions and do not close across a collection gap.
- [ ] Delayed evidence inside a closed window revises the same report; fresh qualifying action beyond it starts a separate report. Preserve revisions rather than silently combining encounters.
- [ ] Context can enrich a report but cannot independently authorize discovery; time/system inclusion must not claim a complete roster or one unified fleet.
- [ ] Verify duplicate, out-of-order, changed and late observations, context preceding the first source match, healthy quiet, unknown health and renewed action through the primary seam.

**Spec coverage:** User Stories 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 22, 33, 40, 41, 53. All accepted evidence, privacy and ordinary-feed-availability constraints apply.
